The problem

Generation is solved.
Knowing which tenth is wrong is not.

01

The confidence score is the problem

Frontier models already reach roughly 91% F1 on attribute extraction, and plenty of vendors will sell you enrichment with a confidence number attached. That number is the model's opinion of its own work — and it runs highest exactly where the model is most certain and most wrong.

02

So nothing actually gets automated

Distributors still pay people to check 100% of records. A tool that is right nine times in ten, with no way to tell which nine, has not removed the review. It has only moved it.

03

An untrusted proposer, checked by tools

Crucible never takes the model's word for anything. Every value it proposes is examined by six independent checks — unit algebra, cross-attribute constraints, controlled vocabularies, identity cross-referencing — and only what survives is published. The rest is handed to you as a queue, with reasons.

Proof

A real catch, from a real run

A ball bearing. The model proposed C4 as the seal type. Here is what each check had to say about it.

BRG-00027 bearing.ball · seal_type proposed C4
ensemble 1.00 Identical across three independent samples. The model agreed with itself every single time.
coherence 1.00 “C4” appears in 10% of this category. Statistically it looks entirely unremarkable.
dimensional abstained seal_type is a nominal attribute, not a physical quantity. Nothing here to check.
constraint abstained No constraint in this category mentions seal_type.
identity abstained Not an identity claim.
vocabulary 0.00 “C4” is not a term seal_type accepts. This category allows open, 2RS rubber sealed both sides, RS rubber sealed one side, 2Z metal shielded both sides, Z metal shielded one side.

C4 is a bearing clearance code, not a seal type. The model was consistent, and consistently wrong. The statistics agreed with it. Only the check that knows what this category actually sells caught it — because a model cannot correct an error it is certain about.

Three checks abstained, and that is reported rather than quietly folded in. “Nothing to check” and “checked and satisfied” are different facts — merging them is how a catalog ends up auto-publishing precisely the values nothing examined.

How it works

Upload a CSV. Get a sheet you can defend.

STEP 01

Upload

Your ERP export. Part numbers, descriptions, brand columns — six columns is plenty.

STEP 02

Classify

Each product is routed to a category, or honestly marked unclassified. No department invented to fill a cell.

STEP 03

Extract

A local model proposes values. Anything it cannot ground in your source text is discarded before you see it.

STEP 04

Verify

Six independent checks examine every value. Each one may pass, fail, doubt — or say it had nothing to check.

STEP 05

Set your risk

One dial: the maximum error rate you can live with. Everything re-thresholds instantly.

STEP 06

Download

The 252-column sheet as XLSX or CSV, plus an evidence file explaining every populated cell.

Typical AI enrichment

  • Fills all 252 columns
  • Attaches a confidence score the model produced about itself
  • You still check every record, because you cannot tell which are wrong
  • A blank cell means the tool gave up

Crucible

  • Fills what the evidence supports
  • Every value carries a source quote and six independent verdicts
  • You check the queue, not the catalog
  • A blank cell is a finding — the attribute applies, and nothing supported a value
